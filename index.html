Pro Tips

Quick Wins Every Time

📋
More transcript = better deck Paste the full recording transcript if you have it. Gemini will pull your customer's exact phrases and use them verbatim — sounds authentic because it is.
🚫
Always name the tools they love Add every tool they currently use to the "Tech Stack" field. The deck will say "HubSpot Integration" not "replace HubSpot" — that's what closes deals.
🔁
Iterate in Gemini freely After Gemini generates the deck, just ask it to change anything. "Make the hero darker," "change Phase 1 to November," "add a slide about their Oracle integration." It updates inline.
📱
Test it on your phone first Open the Netlify URL on your phone before you share. The deck is built to be mobile-responsive — a 30-second check ensures executives see it perfectly on theirs.
